# CAP-279: names an expression binds found alike in every language

*Implements* [FEA-024: expressions as data](../features/FEA-024.md). *Relates to* [CAP-098: dialects over one framework](CAP-098.md), [CAP-226: complete expression dialects](CAP-226.md).

## As built: mbse-expressions

Built in mbse-expressions' retrofit of [the complete dialects](https://github.com/pitaman71/mbse-expressions/blob/main/docs/proposals/dialects/README.md#stages-proposed-once-decided) (its *Retrofit's design*, the binders), for 0.10.0,
in both languages, as the user decided in stage 6: "any expression kind that binds symbol(s) should be modeled as
variations of `let`", "(a)": every binding kind refines the framework's `Let`
([CAP-226: complete expression dialects](CAP-226.md#decided)). The choices made where the design was silent are
[the port's API, Settled in the retrofit](https://github.com/pitaman71/mbse-expressions/blob/main/docs/proposals/port/API.md#settled-in-the-retrofit-for-the-users-review), items 217 to 222 and 242.

- **Every binder refines `Let`**: Patterns' `quantifier`, MATLAB's `arrayfun` and `import`, Excel's `map`, Python3's
  `lambda`, comprehensions, `namedexpr` and imports, TypeScript5's functions, arrows and classes, TypeScript Types'
  mapped, function and constructor types, C++'s lambdas and `requires` expressions, and SystemVerilog's two (stage 6);
  Excel's, LaTeX's and Python3's `let` are the framework's `Let` itself. Validation, inference and free names read
  each as a binder, its bound names its kind's scopes'.
- **Its own properties renamed to Let's** where it holds the one term it binds from or within: `collection` and
  `array` are `value`, a comprehension's `elt` and a `dictcomp`'s `value` are `body`, a mapped type's `constraint` and
  `typeAnnotation` `value` and `body`; Let's properties first. The writers keep their names and argument order.
- **Not binders**: a pattern of any family, which binds through what holds it; a conditional type's `infer`, by its
  scopes; a helper that binds, through its kind; a macro definition, a type parameter and C's expressions.
- **Tests**: DIA-30, a binder of every dialect refining `Let`, its properties and scopes; TST-03; the cases that read
  or write a renamed property under their IDs.

## Decided by an agent, for review

Recorded in [the port's API, Settled in the retrofit](https://github.com/pitaman71/mbse-expressions/blob/main/docs/proposals/port/API.md#settled-in-the-retrofit-for-the-users-review), not marked as the user's: each *proposed* until reviewed.

- **(mbse-expressions, retrofit, API 218)** Each binder's mapping to Let's `name`, `value` and `body` (the design's
  table); an import's `name` stays what it imports, so its wire does not change.
- **(mbse-expressions, retrofit, API 217)** A binder binds by its role: only a `BINDING` kind without scopes reads as a
  let.
- **(mbse-expressions, retrofit, API 219, 220)** Let's unset slots are None in a form and listed in its kind's
  `optional`.
