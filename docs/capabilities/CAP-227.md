# CAP-227: the Python3 expression dialect

*Implements* [FEA-024: expressions as data](../features/FEA-024.md), [FEA-008: expressive program models](../features/FEA-008.md). Index entry: [capabilities/README.md](README.md#CAP-227). *Relates to* [CAP-226: complete expression dialects](CAP-226.md), [CAP-100: six languages' expression dialects](CAP-100.md), [CAP-036: complete kinds for Python, TypeScript and C and C++](CAP-036.md), [CAP-038: every construct of Python's standards](CAP-038.md), [CAP-089: Python expressions bridged to Python source](CAP-089.md).

## Design

Not built. `Python3` is a complete dialect by [CAP-226: complete expression dialects](CAP-226.md)'s rules: every
expression of Python 3, across its minor versions (3.0 to 3.15), as CPython's `ast` has them, each kind with its own
schema, its kinds the branches of `Python3`'s union, `Expressions.Python3.Expression`; printed, and checked against `ast`'s node list. It is today's Python
dialect ([CAP-100: six languages' expression dialects](CAP-100.md)) merged with mbse-programs' Python expression kinds
([CAP-036: complete kinds for Python, TypeScript and C and C++](CAP-036.md)), in mbse-programs' names and shapes; its
statements, and the `match` patterns and type parameters that belong to them, stay mbse-programs'. Reading Python's
every construct is [CAP-038: every construct of Python's standards](CAP-038.md)'s, and the bridge it replaces
[CAP-089: Python expressions bridged to Python source](CAP-089.md)'s.

The gap, as [the proposal's survey](../../../mbse-expressions/docs/proposals/dialects/GAPS.md#python3) counts it: 17 kinds here (12 of the dialect's own and 5 of
the framework's) against `ast`'s 29 expression kinds and 4 helpers (`comprehension`, `arguments`, `arg`,
`keyword`), and mbse-programs' 29 and 11 helpers. Five of ours are narrower than `ast`'s (a comparison of one
operator, `and` and `or` of two operands, a generator of one `for`, a call without keywords, a subscript split in
two) and are replaced; two have no counterpart in Python's grammar (`import` and `importfrom` within an expression)
and are kept, marked as extensions. It is the proposal's stage 3.
