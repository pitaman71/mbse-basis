# CAP-238: container schemas and grid insertion

*Implements* [FEA-056: fungible diagramming keeps agents and humans in the loop](../features/FEA-056.md).

`Framework.Container` models a container's cells, their extents and the children in each, and a grid container places
children without adjacency specs each in its own cell in a predictable sequence, incrementally, in Python and
TypeScript.

## As built

mbse-diagrams' `Framework.Container`, in Python and TypeScript, built by
[pull request 2](https://github.com/pitaman71/mbse-diagrams/pull/2) as [the diagrams proposal](../../proposals/DIAGRAMS.md) states it, with what
Decided by an agent, below, fixes where it is silent; suite CNT tests it in both languages.

## Decided

The user's decisions (2026-11-05), in review of mbse-basis#55:

- **A removed child's gap closes**: "agreed that removing a child causes the gap to close". A grid is always the
  sequence of its children's cells.
- **The container model tracks each child's original insertion position**: "the container model can track original
  insertion position". Each `Container.Holds` entry keeps its child's `order`, from its grid's `inserted` count, through
  every move ([CAP-239: grid adjacency](CAP-239.md)).

The user's decisions (2026-11-06), on physical layout:

- **Containers are implied**: "Containers are implicit constructs that contain shapes and participate in deciding how
  those shapes are arranged with respect to one another." "The diagram is a rectangular root object which has an
  implied container for top-level shapes in the diagram. Every shape may have an implied container and must have a
  container if it has any children (the "children" of a shape are always delegated to its implied container)." A
  grid is made when its container gains its first child.
- **A cell's key is its position**: "for now that's fine"; superseded on 2026-11-11 by its `row` and `column` on
  `Container.HasCells` ([CAP-280: the grid in the logical layout](CAP-280.md)).

## Decided by an agent, for review

Where this capability and the proposal were silent, the simplest default consistent with them, with
[CAP-237: logical layout schemas](CAP-237.md) and with mbse-schemas, each *proposed* until reviewed, labeled with the repository and number
it was decided under.

- **(mbse-diagrams 2)** **Names.** The schemas are named `Container.*`; the install's uid is
  `mbse.Diagrams.Container/1`. It runs after `Logical`'s, and gives `Logical.OfShape` an adjacency, `cell`, and
  `Logical.OfGrid` its cells, rules and counters.
- **(mbse-diagrams 2, mbse-diagrams 11)** **A grid** is a container's, `Logical.OfGrid`, owned by its container, a
  shape or the diagram, and written by mutations ([CAP-280: the grid in the logical layout](CAP-280.md)); the
  container layout takes its settings, its span and axis its own or else the diagram's `grid_span` and `grid_grows`,
  3 and `"rows"` unless set, when it gains its first child or its settings change.
- **(mbse-diagrams 2)** **Its axes.** A grid grows along one axis, `grows`, `"rows"` or `"columns"`, and holds `span`
  cells, a positive int, along the other; that is its elasticity, the proposal's "permit or forbid elasticity along
  axes". Its extents, `rows` and `columns`, are the number of cells along each axis its children occupy: an empty grid
  is 0 by 0.
- **(mbse-diagrams 2, mbse-diagrams 11)** **A cell**, `Container.OfCell`, is owned by its grid through
  `Container.HasCells`, keyed by its `row` and `column`, ints counted from 0, which it holds too: row 0 at the top and
  column 0 at the left, relative to the container's compass. A cell exists only where a child sits. A shape is in one
  cell (`Container.Holds`), and is placed once: placing it again is refused.
- **(mbse-diagrams 2)** **The predictable sequence.** Without adjacency specs, children are placed in the order they
  were added to their container, each keeping that order as its original insertion position: a grid that grows rows
  fills each row, left to right, then the next row down; one that grows columns fills each column, top to bottom, then
  the next column right.
- **(mbse-diagrams 2, mbse-diagrams 4)** **Incremental, by the caller, for now.** Each function adjusts the container
  layout to one mutation of the logical layout, in a transaction of its own, and touches nothing else: `added(child)`
  places a child its container has just gained in the next cell; `removing(child)`, before the mutation that removes
  a child, closes the gap it leaves, each child after it moving back one cell and a cell left empty going. The caller
  calls them until [CAP-243: a diagram displayed from its mutation stream](README.md#CAP-243) drives them from the mutation stream.
