# CAP-166: constraints attached where they constrain

*Implements* [FEA-034: analytic behavior conventions in schema](../features/FEA-034.md). *Relates to* [CAP-025: nothing mandatory but by constraint](CAP-025.md), [PRI-021: refinement by constraint](../PRINCIPLES.md#PRI-021), [FEA-024: expressions as data](../features/FEA-024.md).

## Design

Not designed. Constraints are mbse-schemas' and mbse-expressions' ([PRI-011: the lowest repository that can own it](../PRINCIPLES.md#PRI-011)): a pattern's `requires`, held by mbse-schemas' `OfPattern` ([CAP-137: patterns of symbols, variables and specs](CAP-137.md)), is terms of mbse-expressions' dialects, `Patterns` among them, which mbse-schemas' executor evaluates ([CAP-142: expressions evaluated in queries](CAP-142.md)) through an evaluator given to its store, so that mbse-schemas does not depend on mbse-expressions, as the user decided ([FEA-024: expressions as data](../features/FEA-024.md#decided): the port's 5, and the decision of 2026-11-05 on `requires`). Patterns are kept beside the schemas, not in them, so that several sets may apply to one schema. Where nothing is mandatory but by a constraint ([CAP-025: nothing mandatory but by constraint](CAP-025.md)), the constraints attached to a model are its specification.

## Open questions

- **Where constraints attach.** A specification is constraints attached to the parts of a system they constrain, each
  held by a property named `requires` ([Why the mbse repositories exist](../../MBSE.md#what-a-specification-is-made-of)); mbse-schemas'
  patterns already hold theirs so (`OfPattern.requires`). Which scopes a constraint attaches to (a schema, a property, a relation, an
  interface, a whole specification), and how attached constraints combine into one conjunction, is not decided.
  The elements that bind parameters (see [CAP-112: parameters modeled as schema data](CAP-112.md)) are likely the same scopes.
