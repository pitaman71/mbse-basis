# CAP-241: physical layout schemas

*Implements* [FEA-056: fungible diagramming keeps agents and humans in the loop](../features/FEA-056.md).

`Framework.Physical` models a physical layout's relative and absolute coordinates, annotating a logical layout, in
Python and TypeScript.

## As built

mbse-diagrams' `Framework.Physical`, in Python and TypeScript, built by
[pull request 5](https://github.com/pitaman71/mbse-diagrams/pull/5) as [the diagrams proposal](../../proposals/DIAGRAMS.md) states it, with what
Decided by an agent, below, fixes where it is silent; suite PHY tests it in both languages. Its components are
[CAP-242: incremental physical placement](README.md#CAP-242)'s.

## Decided

The user's decisions (2026-11-06), on physical layout:

- **Diagram units**: "Physical layout is computed in terms of floating point "diagram units"."
- **Unit lengths**: "Every shape, container, cell in the diagram gets its own `unit_length : float` computed from its
  container (and from its container all the way up to the diagram). `scaling_factor` is applied in this process
  between container and its children." The diagram's `unit_length`, "how long a "unit length" vector is at the top
  level of the diagram (1.0 is typical)", and `scaling_factor`, "how "unit length" scales from container to child at
  each level of containment", are its properties: a container and its cells have their shape's unit length, and each
  child that times the scaling factor.
- **The diagram is dynamically sized; the viewport is the UI's**: "The diagram itself is dynamically sized, expanding
  as necessary when cells are added and shrinking when cells are removed from its implied container. A UI component
  should have a fixed-size viewport, and should auto-scale to accommodate diagram resizing specifically when one of the
  viewport boundaries are crossed by the resizing."

## Decided by an agent, for review

Where this capability and the proposal were silent, the simplest default consistent with them, with
[CAP-237: logical layout schemas](CAP-237.md) to [CAP-240: conflicting layout rules ignored and requeued](CAP-240.md)'s decisions and with mbse-schemas, each *proposed* until reviewed, labeled with the
repository and number it was decided under.

- **(mbse-diagrams 5)** **Names.** The schemas are named `Physical.*`; the install's uid is `mbse.Diagrams.Physical/1`.
  It runs after `Logical`'s and `Container`'s, whose schemas it annotates.
- **(mbse-diagrams 5, mbse-diagrams 6)** **Coordinates** are diagram units, floats, `x` to the right and `y` down, as
  a screen's are, so that north, a compass's 0 degrees, is toward −y: `Physical.OfPoint2`, an `x` and a `y`, and
  `Physical.OfSize`, a `width` and a `height`, both value objects.
- **(mbse-diagrams 5, mbse-diagrams 6)** **An annotation is owned by what it annotates**, through `Logical.Owns`, as
  `placement` or `frame`, one each, in its role through a relation of its own, so that it has a path, its owner's, and
  goes when its owner does: the proposal's "annotation relations".
- **(mbse-diagrams 5)** **A point's placement**, `Physical.OfPlacement`, holds its `absolute` coordinates, in the
  viewport, its `relative` coordinates, from the point it is measured from (its shape's origin for a border or extent
  point, its enclosing cell's frame's top left for an origin), and its `direction`, its compass resolved to an absolute
  direction: the proposal's "relative and absolute coordinates for points, directions".
- **(mbse-diagrams 6)** **Frames**: a diagram's, a shape's, a grid's and a cell's `Physical.OfFrame`, a rectangle's top
  left, `absolute` and `relative` to the frame that encloses it, its `size`, and its `unit_length`. The diagram's frame
  is its size, which grows and shrinks with its cells; the viewport, the UI's, is not modeled.

## Open questions

- **What physical layout writes.** Whether CAP-242 keeps every point's absolute coordinates as data, or derives them
  from the relative ones, and the frames, when it renders.
