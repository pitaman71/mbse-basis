# CAP-241: physical layout schemas

*Implements* [FEA-056: fungible diagramming keeps agents and humans in the loop](../features/FEA-056.md).

`Framework.Physical` models a physical layout's relative and absolute coordinates, annotating a logical layout, in
Python and TypeScript.

## As built

mbse-diagrams' `Framework.Physical`, in Python and TypeScript, built by
[pull request 5](https://github.com/pitaman71/mbse-diagrams/pull/5) as [the diagrams proposal](../../proposals/DIAGRAMS.md) states it, with what
Decided by an agent, below, fixes where it is silent; suite PHY tests it in both languages. Its components are
[CAP-242: incremental physical placement](README.md#CAP-242)'s.

## Decided by an agent, for review

Where this capability and the proposal were silent, the simplest default consistent with them, with
[CAP-237: logical layout schemas](CAP-237.md) to [CAP-240: conflicting layout rules ignored and requeued](CAP-240.md)'s decisions and with mbse-schemas, each *proposed* until reviewed, labeled with the
repository and number it was decided under.

- **(mbse-diagrams 5)** **Names.** The schemas are named `Physical.*`; the install's uid is `mbse.Diagrams.Physical/1`.
  It runs after `Logical`'s and `Container`'s, whose schemas it annotates.
- **(mbse-diagrams 5)** **Coordinates** are pixels, floats, `x` to the right and `y` down, as a screen's are, so that
  north, a compass's 0 degrees, is toward −y: `Physical.OfPoint2`, an `x` and a `y`, and `Physical.OfSize`, a `width`
  and a `height`, both value objects.
- **(mbse-diagrams 5)** **An annotation is owned by what it annotates**, keyless, one each, through a relation of its
  own, so that it has a path, its owner's, and goes when its owner does: the proposal's "annotation relations".
- **(mbse-diagrams 5)** **A point's placement**, `Physical.OfPlacement`, holds its `absolute` coordinates, in the
  viewport, its `relative` coordinates, from the point it is measured from (its shape's origin for a border or extent
  point, its enclosing cell's frame's top left for an origin), and its `direction`, its compass resolved to an absolute
  direction: the proposal's "relative and absolute coordinates for points, directions".
- **(mbse-diagrams 5)** **Frames**: a grid's and a cell's `Physical.OfFrame`, a rectangle's top left, `absolute` and
  `relative` to the frame that encloses it, and its `size`; and a diagram's `Physical.OfViewport`, its `size`. Shapes
  have no frame of their own: a shape is its points, and its extent points bound it.

## Open questions

- **Units.** Pixels suit a screen; a printed or exported diagram may want another unit, or a scale on the viewport.
- **What physical layout writes.** Whether CAP-242 keeps every point's absolute coordinates as data, or derives them
  from the relative ones, and the frames, when it renders.
