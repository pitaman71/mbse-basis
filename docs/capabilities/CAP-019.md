# CAP-019 · adjacencies at the end of a detail

From the workspace's `VISUALIZATION.md`. *Implements* [FEA-005](../features/FEA-005.md). Index entry:
[capabilities/README.md](README.md#CAP-019).

An object's adjacencies are shown only in its detail (CAP-007), after its
properties, in its schema's declared order: comments last where a schema
declares them last. Where a chip is expected, only chips are shown: no
adjacency list is nested under a chip, in a browser or in an adjacency
list. Deeper levels are reached by inspecting a linked object: a topic's
detail ends with its threads, and a thread's detail with its replies.

An adjacency is previewed as a single chip per entry, divided into two
columns. The left column shows the entry's properties. The right column
shows a pill `{object key} : {object label}` of the linked object, which
is clicked to inspect it. An object without a label of its own (a
comment) is named by its summary, else its first text property, shown as
prose. Where the object has a creation identity and datestamp (CAP-020),
the pill is headed by `{identity} @ {datestamp}`, in italics and a reduced
font.

Adjacencies that are part of the object's ownership path may be shown
abbreviated in breadcrumb form.
