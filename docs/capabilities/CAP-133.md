# CAP-133: no durable references to counted objects

*Implements* [FEA-026: deterministic object identity](../features/FEA-026.md). Index entry: [capabilities/README.md](README.md#CAP-133).

## Design

Not built: the executor still finds what an upsert made by its frame, key and symbol ([CAP-139: upserts find before they create](CAP-139.md)); retiring that is tech debt (Decided).

A counted object has no identity beyond the document that writes it, and nothing finds it but through what links it
(Object identity, Decided, 40 and 55 in [CAP-118: singletons, owned and counted objects](CAP-118.md#decided)). Decided 61 and 63, the identity part of 102, and the `$ref`
of an upsert-made counted object, `{"$ref": {"frame", "key", "symbol"}}`, give it the durable identity that by design
should not exist; a re-run finds what an upsert made by it (TXN-03, "a counted one by its frame, key and symbol").
References ([CAP-130: references to objects, entries and frames](CAP-130.md)) already give counted objects no reference. Retiring the rest: remove the executor's index
and, if nothing else needs it, the upsert's `key`, and rewrite the cases that rely on them.

## Open questions

- **What an upsert of a counted object does when run again**: always create; find it by its spec; or be idempotent
  only through ownership, a model giving such objects an owner.

## Decided

As the user decided: adopted, each the rule as stated, quoted where the wording matters.

- **(references 2)** First, an upserted object's reference was its frame, the upsert's key and the symbol ("as you drafted"); then, on
  finding that Decided 61 and 63 "are trying to create durable references to counted objects that by design should
  not be necessary or exist": "remove References.OfUpserted entirely from the prototype". "the purpose of this phase
  is to create durable references for client code": the executor keeps finding what an upsert made by its frame, key
  and symbol (61, 63, 102 in [CAP-139: upserts find before they create](CAP-139.md#decided)), and retiring that is tech debt, "log tech debt to eradicate durable direct references to
  counted objects" (this capability).
