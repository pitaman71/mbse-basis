# CAP-231: the SystemVerilog expression dialect, complete

*Implements* [FEA-024: expressions as data](../features/FEA-024.md), [FEA-008: expressive program models](../features/FEA-008.md). Index entry: [capabilities/README.md](README.md#CAP-231). *Relates to* [CAP-226: complete expression dialects](CAP-226.md), [CAP-100: six languages' expression dialects](CAP-100.md), [CAP-063: complete kinds for Verilog and SystemVerilog](CAP-063.md), [CAP-094: every construct of Verilog's and SystemVerilog's standards](CAP-094.md), [CAP-090: SystemVerilog expressions bridged to Verilog source](CAP-090.md).

## Design

Not built. `SystemVerilog` becomes complete by [CAP-226: complete expression dialects](CAP-226.md)'s rules: every
expression of IEEE 1800-2023, Verilog (IEEE 1364-2005) its subset, each kind recording the standards that have it,
with the sublanguages expressions hold (patterns, sequences and properties), held by `SystemVerilog`'s own
`Expression`; printed, and checked against slang's node list. It completes today's SystemVerilog dialect
([CAP-100: six languages' expression dialects](CAP-100.md)) with mbse-programs' Verilog expression kinds
([CAP-063: complete kinds for Verilog and SystemVerilog](CAP-063.md)). Reading its every construct is
[CAP-094: every construct of Verilog's and SystemVerilog's standards](CAP-094.md)'s, and the bridge it replaces
[CAP-090: SystemVerilog expressions bridged to Verilog source](CAP-090.md)'s.

The gap, as [the proposal's survey](../../../mbse-expressions/docs/proposals/dialects/GAPS.md#systemverilog) counts it: 17 kinds here (14 of the dialect's own and
the framework's `literal`, `variable` and `call`) against mbse-programs' 40 and about 20 helpers, and the
sublanguages beside them (4 patterns, 9 properties, 6 sequences, 5 timing controls, 8 constraint kinds, 10 data
types). Lacking here: the literals' kinds and spellings, increments and assignments within expressions, assignment
patterns, streaming concatenations, `tagged`, `matches` and `&&&`, `dist`, `this`, `super`, `$`,
`$root`, `{}` and `new`, `randomize() with`, min:typ:max, casts to any data type, and
sequences and properties. It is the proposal's stage 6.
