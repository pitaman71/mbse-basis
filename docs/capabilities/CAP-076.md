# CAP-076: reruns reuse decisions

*Implements* [FEA-059: transforms of models, applied step by step and certified](../features/README.md#FEA-059), [FEA-017: AI in the loop via the model trace](../features/README.md#FEA-017), [FEA-018: humans in the loop via the model trace](../features/README.md#FEA-018).

A session given an earlier history certificate ([CAP-075: steps linked to what they matched and wrote](CAP-075.md))
reuses the parameters of its transform certificates wherever the same transform applies to the same frame, reports the
earlier transform certificates that no longer apply (orphans), and compares two histories (the transforms added,
removed and changed). Who decided each step's parameters, including a reuse, is recorded by the history certificate.
This is what mbse-transforms' first release builds
([its first-release proposal](https://github.com/pitaman71/mbse-transforms/blob/main/docs/proposals/first-release/README.md)).

## Design

What the user decided is stated under Decided, below; the rest is mbse-transforms' first-release proposal, each part
*proposed* until reviewed (Decided by an agent, for review).

- **A step's key** is its transform and its frames, each frame by its symbols' object references (its
  `References.OfFrame`). Two transform certificates match by key.
- **Decisions survive.** A session given an earlier history certificate answers a step's parameters, before any policy,
  from the earlier transform certificate with the same key, and its history certificate records the answer as reused.
  This is always sound: a reuse only replaces asking with an answer that someone gave before, for the same choice about
  the same elements.
- **Orphans**: an earlier transform certificate whose key no candidate has is reported for review, never silently
  dropped.
- **History diff**: two histories are compared by key, and the comparison lists the transforms added, removed, and
  applied with other parameters, each with its decision's provenance.

## As built: mbse-patterns

mbse-patterns 0.8 to 0.9.1 (TRF-08) reused earlier decisions by a step's key, its transform and the object references
of its match, reported the orphans, and diffed two traces by key. That precursor is the design input of
mbse-transforms, not code to port. It is retired: mbse-patterns 0.10.0, its fresh start, removes its code.

## Open questions

- **References that survive renames.** A renamed schema changes every object reference under it, which orphans its
  decisions. Should a source diff detect renames (same content, new name) and carry decisions over, or should the
  person confirm?

## Decided

As the user decided: adopted, each the rule as stated, quoted where the wording matters.

- **Reruns in mbse-transforms' first release**, with orphans and diffs, as the user decided, accepting the
  recommendation ([mbse-transforms' charter](https://github.com/pitaman71/mbse-transforms/blob/main/docs/proposals/charter/README.md#decided),
  Decided, 3): the precursor met it, and mbse-codegen-python uses it.
- **Provenance on the history certificate**: "the history certificate can capture provenance"
  ([the first-release proposal](https://github.com/pitaman71/mbse-transforms/blob/main/docs/proposals/first-release/README.md#decided),
  Decided, 2). Who decided a step's parameters (the caller, a policy, or an earlier history reused) is recorded by the
  history certificate, not by the transform certificate.
- **Parameters on the transform certificate**: "ofc those parameters must be recorded in the transform certificate"
  ([CAP-075: steps linked to what they matched and wrote](CAP-075.md#decided), Decided). A rerun reuses them from
  there.
- **Python first**, as for all of mbse-transforms' first release
  ([CAP-074: transforms applied step by step](CAP-074.md#decided), Decided).

## Decided by an agent, for review

Each is *proposed* until reviewed.

- **(mbse-transforms 3)** **The design above** where the user's decisions leave it open: a step's key as its transform
  and its frames' object references, reuse before any policy, orphans and history diffs by key.
- **(mbse-transforms 3)** **Provenance recorded as mbse-schemas' Provenance records a transaction's**, keyed by the
  step.
