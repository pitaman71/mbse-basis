# CAP-113: parameters bound at their definitions

*Implements* [FEA-025: schema-driven data](../features/FEA-025.md). Index entry: [capabilities/README.md](README.md#CAP-113).

## Design

Not built. `Parametrics.BindsValue` and `BindsObject` are defined ([CAP-112: parameters declared by an installed module](CAP-112.md)), but no kind has an adjacency to either, so nothing binds a parameter: an extension's adjacency is to a relation of two links and no properties ([CAP-117: extension adjacencies to any relation](CAP-117.md)), and the bindings' adjacencies were dropped (Open questions).

## Open questions

### The bindings' adjacencies

*Settled*: **the bindings' adjacencies, `values` and `objects`, go**, as the user (2026-10-22): "lines 30 and 31 of docs/PARAMETRICS.md are no longer needed since `Parametrics.OfParameters` was introduced": a kind's one extension
adjacency is `parameters`. Stopped on before that, for a decision: the extension
mechanism refuses them: an adjacency an extension adds to a meta-schema is to a relation of two links and no
properties (Schema extension, Decided, 18 in [CAP-116: framework schemas extended, checked](CAP-116.md#decided), as built, and 22: "yes that's a compromise"), and `BindsValue` has an
entry property and `BindsObject` three links (Decided, 3), so the draft's install raises `TypeError: an extension's
adjacency 'values' is to a relation of two links and no properties, got 'Parametrics.BindsValue'`. `BindsValue` and
`BindsObject` are defined; no kind has `values` or `objects`. The choices: (a) extend the mechanism, so that a
builder's member of an extension's adjacency holds entries with their properties and links, as a proxy's adjacency
does (`o.values(lambda a: a.insert(lambda e: e.param([n]).value(3)))`), revising Schema extension, Decided, 18 in [CAP-116: framework schemas extended, checked](CAP-116.md#decided) and
22; (b) bindings as objects linked by relations of two links, as formals are (Schema extension, Decided, 22 in [FEA-024: expressions as data](../features/FEA-024.md#decided)); (c)
leave bindings unbuilt until something binds (Store overhaul, Decided, 12 and 28).

## Decided

As the user decided: adopted, each the rule as stated, quoted where the wording matters.

- **(parametrics 3)** **Bindings**, "yes": `BindsObject`'s `obj_ref` is a third link, `BindsValue`'s `value` an entry property.
