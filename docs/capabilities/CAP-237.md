# CAP-237: logical layout schemas

*Implements* [FEA-056: fungible diagramming keeps agents and humans in the loop](../features/FEA-056.md).

`Framework.Logical` models a diagram's logical layout, in Python and TypeScript: points, vectors, rotations, compasses
and shapes with their origin, border and extent points, and containment and adjacency between shapes.

## Design

As [the diagrams proposal](../../proposals/DIAGRAMS.md) states it; mbse-diagrams'
[pull request 1](https://github.com/pitaman71/mbse-diagrams/pull/1) builds it, `Framework.Logical` in both languages,
with suite LOG.

## Decided

The user's decisions (2026-11-05), in review of mbse-basis#51:

- **Points are owned, not counted**: "points are owned not counted". "Each point is owned via the adjacency to a
  shape": its origin through `Logical.Origin`, keyless, a shape having one; a border or extent point through
  `Logical.Borders` or `Logical.Extents`, keyed by a `name` unique within its shape. Every point is therefore a shape's
  origin, border or extent point.
- **Shapes and diagrams are owned too**, since mbse-schemas owns nothing by a counted object, as the agent proposed and
  the user "agreed": a diagram, `Logical.OfDiagram`, is owned by the singleton directory `Logical.Diagrams`, keyed by its
  `name`; a top-level shape by its diagram (`Logical.InDiagram`), a child shape by its container
  (`Logical.Containment`), each keyed by its `name`. Every point and shape has a path, such as `Logical.Diagrams`,
  diagram `overview`, shape `cpu`, border `ne`; moving a shape to another container changes its identity.

## Decided by an agent, for review

Where this capability and the proposal were silent, the simplest default consistent with them and with mbse-schemas,
each *proposed* until reviewed, labeled with the repository and number it was decided under.

- **(mbse-diagrams 1)** **Names.** The schemas are named `Logical.*` (`Logical.OfPoint`, `Logical.Vector`, ...), as
  mbse-schemas names its own modules' (`Provenance.*`); the install's uid is `mbse.Diagrams.Logical/1`.
- **(mbse-diagrams 1)** **A rotation** is a value object, `Logical.OfRotation`, of one property, `degrees`, a float,
  clockwise from the reference compass's north. Compasses compose by adding degrees, modulo 360, into [0, 360).
- **(mbse-diagrams 1)** **A point** is a reference object with no properties of its own: a name and nothing more, as
  the proposal says; its key is its owning entry's.
- **(mbse-diagrams 1)** **A vector** is an entry of the relation `Logical.Vector`, linking `start` to `end`, with a
  `rotation`; `unique(rotation)`: one rotation per start and end.
- **(mbse-diagrams 1)** **Border points keep the order they were added in**, as every adjacency does.
- **(mbse-diagrams 1)** **Adjacency** is the relation `Logical.Adjacency`, of `from_shape` and `to_shape`, with a
  `rotation`, and no link to their container, which is the one they share. Its links are not named `from` and `to`:
  `from` is a keyword in Python, and a builder's setter is named after its link.
- **(mbse-diagrams 1)** **Bounds are validated on request.** The `unique` clauses are upper bounds that mbse-schemas'
  validators report, as for any relation; builders do not refuse an entry that breaks one.
- **(mbse-diagrams 1)** **`compass(x)`** resolves a point's or a shape's logical compass by the proposal's rules. Until
  container layout models cells ([CAP-238: container schemas and grid insertion](README.md#CAP-238)), and as no container, cell or shape rotates, an
  origin's compass is north, and a border or extent point's is the rotation of the vector from its shape's origin. It
  raises `ValueError` where the rules leave a compass undefined: a shape without an origin, or a point of one; a point
  with no vector from its shape's origin, or more than one; a vector without a rotation.

## Open questions

- **Structural rules as constraints.** That a border or extent point's vector starts at its shape's origin, and that
  adjacent shapes share a container, are rules `compass` and layout assume, which no validator checks yet.
