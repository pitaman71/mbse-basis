# CAP-237: logical layout schemas

*Implements* [FEA-056: fungible diagramming keeps agents and humans in the loop](../features/FEA-056.md).

`Framework.Logical` models a diagram's logical layout, in Python and TypeScript: points, vectors, rotations, compasses
and shapes with their origin, border and extent points, and containment and adjacency between shapes.

## As built

mbse-diagrams' `Framework.Logical`, in Python and TypeScript, built by
[pull request 1](https://github.com/pitaman71/mbse-diagrams/pull/1) as [the diagrams proposal](../../proposals/DIAGRAMS.md) states it, with what
Decided and Decided by an agent, below, fix where it is silent; suite LOG tests it in both languages.

## Decided

The user's decisions (2026-11-05), in review of mbse-basis#51:

- **Every diagram element is owned, none counted**: "points are owned not counted", and, in review of mbse-basis#69,
  "All diagram elements - points, vectors, segments, shapes, diagram are all owned", through the one ownership
  relation below; an adjacency spec, also an element, is owned by its `from_shape`. A diagram is owned by the singleton
  `Logical.Diagrams`, a shape by its diagram or its container, and a point, vector, segment or spec by its shape, so
  that every element has a path, from `Logical.Diagrams`, through diagram `overview`, to shape `cpu` and its point
  `ne`; moving a shape to another container changes its identity.
- **One ownership relation** (2026-11-06): "Let's simplify ownership under one relation `Owns`", `Logical.Owns`, of
  `owner` and `owned`, keyed by a `name` unique among its owner's. It "only lives in `Logical`": `Container` and
  `Physical` own theirs through it, found by name.
- **Role relations stay**: "No, we are not dropping role relations." What part an object plays (a diagram's top-level
  shapes, a container's children, a shape's origin, border and extent points, a vector's start and end, a segment's
  ends, a spec's shapes) is a relation of its own beside `Owns`, which owns nothing.
- **Relations are not reference objects**: "This model is starting to confuse relations with reference objects." What
  has properties of its own, or is referred to, annotated or drawn, is a reference object, linked by role relations: a
  vector, `Logical.OfVector`; a segment; an adjacency spec, `Logical.OfAdjacency`, owned by its `from_shape`.
- **Vectors have a length**: "Vectors have `length : float` in terms of diagram units", in units of the shape's level.
- **Segments**: "A new element type `Segment` is attached to two points and has no stated length", `Logical.OfSegment`,
  its `ends` the two points.

## Decided by an agent, for review

Where this capability and the proposal were silent, the simplest default consistent with them and with mbse-schemas,
each *proposed* until reviewed, labeled with the repository and number it was decided under.

- **(mbse-diagrams 1)** **Names.** The schemas are named `Logical.*` (`Logical.OfPoint`, `Logical.Vector`, ...), as
  mbse-schemas names its own modules' (`Provenance.*`); the install's uid is `mbse.Diagrams.Logical/1`.
- **(mbse-diagrams 1)** **A rotation** is a value object, `Logical.OfRotation`, of one property, `degrees`, a float,
  clockwise from the reference compass's north. Compasses compose by adding degrees, modulo 360, into [0, 360).
- **(mbse-diagrams 1, mbse-diagrams 6)** **A point** is a reference object with no properties of its own: a name and
  nothing more, as the proposal says; its key is its `Logical.Owns` entry's name, `origin` for a shape's origin.
- **(mbse-diagrams 6)** **A vector**, `Logical.OfVector`, is owned by the shape whose points it joins, its `start`
  and `end` one point each (`Logical.VectorStart`, `Logical.VectorEnd`), its `rotation` and its `length`, a float.
- **(mbse-diagrams 1)** **Border points keep the order they were added in**, as every adjacency does.
- **(mbse-diagrams 1, mbse-diagrams 6)** **An adjacency spec**, `Logical.OfAdjacency`, is owned by its `from_shape`,
  named for its `to_shape` and rotation (`to fpu 90`), with one `from_shape` and one `to_shape`
  (`Logical.AdjacencyFrom`, `Logical.AdjacencyTo`) and a `rotation`. The links are not named `from` and `to`: `from` is a keyword in Python.
- **(mbse-diagrams 6)** **A segment's two ends** are entries of `Logical.SegmentEnds`, which bounds them by no clause:
  that a segment has exactly two is a constraint no validator checks yet.
- **(mbse-diagrams 1)** **Bounds are validated on request.** The `unique` clauses are upper bounds that mbse-schemas'
  validators report, as for any relation; builders do not refuse an entry that breaks one.
- **(mbse-diagrams 1)** **`compass(x)`** resolves a point's or a shape's logical compass by the proposal's rules: a
  shape's is its origin's, and an origin's its enclosing cell's, which is its container's, turned by the cell rotation
  of its container's grid where container layout gives it a cell ([CAP-239: grid adjacency](CAP-239.md#decided)), and so on to the
  viewport, whose compass is north; a border or extent point's is its origin's, turned by the rotation of the vector
  from its shape's origin. It raises `ValueError` where the rules leave a compass undefined: a shape without an origin,
  or a point of one; a point with no vector from its shape's origin, or more than one; a vector without a rotation.

## Open questions

- **Structural rules as constraints.** That a border or extent point's vector starts at its shape's origin, and that
  adjacent shapes share a container, are rules `compass` and layout assume, which no validator checks yet.
