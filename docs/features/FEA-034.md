# FEA-034: analytic behavior conventions in schema

*Confers* [QUA-008: usable without code](../QUALITIES.md#QUA-008). Index entry: [features/README.md](README.md#FEA-034).

Data of any schema is compared, ordered, hashed and validated by conventions its schema declares, never by the host language's own.

## Capabilities

- [CAP-111: equality and ordering defined by the schema](../capabilities/CAP-111.md): met
- [CAP-147: links validated against their schemas](../capabilities/CAP-147.md): not met
- [CAP-160: data validated against its schema on request](../capabilities/CAP-160.md): met
- [CAP-166: constraints attached where they constrain](../capabilities/CAP-166.md): not met
